# Design Refinement Notes — First Model → Mk1

**Date:** 2026-09-18

## Observations from Original Sketches

### Top Views (Multi-rotor concepts)
- Multiple arms radiating from central body
- Circular elements at arm tips = ducted fans or propellers
- Suggests a quadrotor or hex-rotor lift system mounted above or integrated into the chassis

### Side Profiles
- Streamlined elongated body (motorcycle DNA)
- Clear rear propeller / ducted fan for forward thrust
- Front fairing / nose
- Wheel-like elements front and rear (hybrid ground capability)
- Seating position appears mid-body, slightly reclined or upright motorcycle style

## Recommended Refinements for Mk1

1. **Lift System**  
   Prefer 4–6 ducted electric fans for redundancy and quieter operation. Position them symmetrically around the center of gravity.

2. **Thrust System**  
   Keep the prominent rear ducted propeller shown in the second sketch. This gives efficient forward flight and matches the motorcycle aesthetic.

3. **Chassis**  
   Motorcycle-style backbone or twin-spar frame. Wheels remain for ground mode and as emergency landing gear.

4. **Cockpit**  
   Open or semi-open with minimal fairing for weight and visibility. Handlebar controls preferred over sticks for motorcycle feel.

5. **Target Specs (initial proposal)**  
   - Empty mass: < 80 kg  
   - Pilot + payload: up to 100 kg  
   - Hover endurance: 15–25 minutes  
   - Max level speed: 80–120 km/h  
   - Dual mode: hover + limited ground roll

6. **Next Drawing Tasks**  
   - Clean side view with dimensions  
   - Top view showing rotor placement  
   - Front view for width and ground clearance

These notes will guide the first technical drawings and component selection.
