# Monad Airbike Mk1 — Concept Freeze

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** Concept frozen under autonomous automation directive  
**Owner:** Eric Allen Kidd / Eternal Unity Tech Empire

## Locked Decisions

### Configuration
- Hybrid personal air-ground vehicle (hoverbike)
- Primary lift: 4–6 electric ducted fans arranged for VTOL and hover stability
- Forward thrust: rear ducted propeller / thruster (motorcycle aesthetic from original sketches)
- Ground mode: retained wheels for rolling and emergency landing
- 30-minute total flight time achieved via hybrid power (battery + micro-turbine / range extender option)

### Power System
- High-power NMC battery pack (Tesla-inspired tabless cylindrical cells preferred)
- Usable battery energy: 8–12 kWh (keeps mass reasonable)
- Active liquid cooling
- Optional micro-turbine or small generator for sustained endurance beyond pure battery
- Continuous power target: 30–40 kW class

### Structure & Materials (Mostly Plastic)
- Primary frame: continuous carbon-fiber reinforced thermoplastic
- Body panels / fairings / seat: carbon-filled nylon or impact-modified engineering plastics
- High-heat zones: CF-PEEK / PEKK family
- Metal minimized to motor mounts, axles, and critical hard points only
- Goal: lightest possible flight-worthy structure matching original notebook sketches

### Control & Interface
- Open-source flight stack (ArduPilot or PX4) with VTOL + hover hold
- Motorcycle-style handlebars and seating position
- Mode switch: Air / Ground / Hybrid
- Automatic stabilization for beginner-friendly flight

### Performance Targets (Mk1)
- Empty mass target: as low as practical with plastic composites (stretch still under 100 kg)
- Pilot + payload: up to 100 kg
- Flight endurance: 30 minutes total (hover + cruise mix)
- Dual-mode operation

## What Is Now Ready
- Concept is frozen enough to begin detailed structural layout and component sourcing
- All major trade-offs (endurance vs mass, pure electric vs hybrid, metal vs plastic) have been decided in favor of a light, mostly-plastic hybrid that can still deliver 30 minutes of flight
