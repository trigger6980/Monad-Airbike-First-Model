## Locked Decision

**Hybrid power system** is the official Mk1 choice to achieve 30-minute flight time while keeping the vehicle light and mostly plastic.

- Battery: 8–12 kWh high-power NMC pack (Tesla-inspired tabless cylindrical preferred)
- Pack mass target: 30–45 kg
- Role: high-power source for takeoff, hover, landing, and emergency pure-electric flight
- Range extender: micro-turbine / small generator option for sustained cruise and full 30+ min endurance
- Cooling: active liquid mandatory
- Continuous battery power: 30–40 kW class

This decision preserves the original light-weight, motorcycle-like character of the sketches while still meeting the 30-minute fly time requirement.
